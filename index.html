Ganesh 25th Anniversary

25 Years of Blessings, Memories & Togetherness

After our marriage in January 2001, we decided to celebrate Ganesh Chaturthi in our home in Piscataway, New Jersey. What began as a small and heartfelt prayer soon grew into a beautiful tradition that brought together family, friends, laughter, devotion, and countless cherished memories. Over the past 25 years, this celebration has become more than an event for us. It has been a journey of togetherness, blessings, and love, shared with everyone who has walked this path with us.
